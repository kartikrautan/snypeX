# snypeX

### Be early to the conversation that changes your pipeline.

Posting into an empty feed is hard work with very little upside. **snypeX** helps founders find the conversations that are already gaining momentum on X, then gives them a sharp, human-reviewed way to join before the comments fill up.

It is not an auto-spam bot. It is an inbound distribution copilot for people who want attention without sacrificing credibility.

## The moment that matters

Most replies arrive after the conversation is over. snypeX focuses on the narrow window when a post is moving fast but the thread is still open:

1. **Spot the breakout** - Find high-signal posts in your niche while they are still accelerating.
2. **Choose your angle** - Compare three reply strategies built for authority, proof, or useful disagreement.
3. **Make it yours** - Review, edit, and add your own voice before anything leaves your hands.
4. **Join natively** - Open a prepared reply in X with one click and start a real conversation.

## Why founders use it

- **Breakout Velocity Radar** surfaces posts gaining traction in their first 90 minutes.
- **Three-Angle Reply Engine** turns one opportunity into three considered ways to contribute.
- **Anti-Slop Filter** rejects empty reactions and generic AI phrasing.
- **Lead Question Hunter** finds conversations where people are actively looking for a solution.
- **Human-in-the-loop workflow** keeps you in control of every reply and every post.

## The principle

**Earn attention by being useful at the exact moment people are paying attention.**

snypeX is designed to help you participate in the right conversation, with a reply worth reading, while keeping the judgment and the voice human.
